How the rule works: As a visa holder for short stays, Tuyen may spend at most 90 days within any rolling 180-day period in the entire Schengen area combined. Both the day of entry and the day of exit count as full days of stay. The 3-year multi-entry visa allows repeated entries, but never more than 90/180. This tool is a planning aid, not legal advice — always verify with the official EU short-stay calculator before travelling.
